NEXUS ENTERPRISE CORP - HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY MANUAL (v4.2)
EFFECTIVE DATE: JANUARY 1, 2026

1.0 PAID TIME OFF (PTO) ALLOCATION
All full-time employees accrue 22 days of Paid Time Off per calendar year. Part-time employees accrue PTO on a pro-rata basis calculated at 1.2 days per 100 hours worked. 
* Rollover Policy: A maximum of 5 unused PTO days can be rolled over into the next calendar year. Any additional accrued, unused PTO above 5 days will be automatically forfeited on December 31st at midnight.
* Approval Workflow: Any PTO request exceeding 3 consecutive business days requires departmental VP approval at least 14 days in advance. Requests under 3 days only require immediate manager sign-off via the HR portal.

2.0 REMOTE WORK & EQUIPMENT ALLOWANCE
Nexus Enterprise Corp operates under a hybrid model. Employees are expected in the physical office on Tuesdays and Thursdays. 
* Home Office Stipend: Full-time employees are eligible for a one-time remote work setup stipend of $500. This stipend covers ergonomic chairs, monitors, and computer peripherals. 
* Reimbursement Process: Expense reports must be submitted via ExpenseBot within 30 days of purchase with valid receipts attached. Submissions after 30 days will be permanently rejected.
